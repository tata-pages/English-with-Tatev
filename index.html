My Education & Professional Background

Hello! My name is Tatevik Sahakyan, and I have been teaching English for more than 12 years. Throughout my teaching career, I have helped hundreds of students improve their English skills, achieve their target TOEFL scores, gain admission to international universities, and communicate confidently in both academic and professional environments.

I graduated from Yerevan State University, Faculty of Romance and Germanic Philology, where I earned my Bachelor's degree in English. I later completed my Master's Degree in English, which strengthened my knowledge of language acquisition, linguistics, and modern teaching methodology.

To provide internationally recognized teaching standards, I successfully completed the prestigious Cambridge CELTA (Certificate in English Language Teaching to Adults). I also hold all three Cambridge Teaching Knowledge Test (TKT) certificates, covering language teaching methodology, lesson planning, classroom management and assessment.

I specialize in:

  • ✔ General English
  • ✔ TOEFL Preparation
  • ✔ Financial English
  • ✔ Business English
  • ✔ Speaking Fluency
  • ✔ Grammar & Vocabulary Development
  • ✔ Academic Writing

Every lesson is carefully planned according to each student's level, learning style and personal goals. I strongly believe that learning English should be practical, motivating and enjoyable. My lessons combine clear explanations, interactive practice and continuous feedback so that every student can see real progress.

Whether you are preparing for the TOEFL exam, improving your English for work, or simply becoming a more confident speaker, I will support and guide you throughout your learning journey.
